QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do all widgets fit in the notch without sideways scrolling?

Yes. OpenNook automatically scales every widget to fit side-by-side inside the notch at once. You can switch between Compact, Standard, and Large sizes in Settings (or directly from the top-right size button in the notch) and the widgets dynamically resize to match.

Does my Mac need a notch?

No. On screens without a physical camera notch, a slim handle at the top center of the screen opens the exact same Nook and Tray.

Does it hide from the Dock and Cmd+Tab App Switcher?

Yes. OpenNook runs as a native background accessory app with a menu bar icon only—never showing an app icon in your Dock or when switching apps with Cmd+Tab.

Does OpenNook collect any personal data or require a paid license?

Never. OpenNook is 100% free, open source, and runs locally on your Mac with zero telemetry or cloud accounts.
